Why people switch.

The reasons come up again and again when someone moves their screenshot workflow here.

Open a tab and start. No trial clock, no per-seat pricing, no app to keep updated.

Frames, backgrounds, annotations, color, AI, store sets, and video all live in the same canvas.

Editing runs on your machine. Your images never leave unless you reach for a cloud feature.
