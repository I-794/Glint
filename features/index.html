Frames and backgrounds.

Brand Kit

Style and effects.

Cinematic grain with blend modes, plus one-tap Noir, Vintage, Vivid, and Faded looks.

Soft shadows, mirrored reflections, 3D tilt, and a focus spotlight, all baked into exports.

Arrows, lines, ellipses, triangles, polygons, stars, freehand, numbered callouts, and stickers.

Pixelate or blur sensitive regions, and dim everything but the area you want people to look at.
