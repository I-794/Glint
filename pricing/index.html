Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Glint is open source and runs in your browser. Every tool in the studio is free and stays free.

What is Glint Pro?

A paid plan we are working on for the parts of Glint that cost money to run: hosted AI, share links and syncing across devices. It is not out yet and has no price yet. Nothing in the studio today moves behind it, and bringing your own API key keeps working. Join the waitlist above and we will send one email when it opens.

Do I need an account?

No. Your work stays in your browser. Sign-in is optional and only enables cloud sync and sharing across devices.

What needs a connection?

Generative AI edits and share links. You can point those at the hosted backend or your own API key, and skip them entirely if you prefer to stay local.

Where does my data go?

Nothing uploads unless you ask. See the privacy page for the full breakdown of what stays local and what leaves.
