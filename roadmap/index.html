Roadmap

Where the studio is headed.

Glint ships in the open, one real need at a time. Here is a directional view of what has landed, what is in motion, and what we are still thinking through.

This is a direction, not a set of dated promises. Priorities shift as we learn what people actually need, and the changelog is the record of what has actually shipped.

Shipped

  • Share links and branching toursPublic /s/<id> links for designs, tours, galleries and comparisons, with expiring links and view counts.
  • Glint Pro sync and accountsOptional sign-in, hosted AI credits, and cross-device sync for projects, looks, brand kits, templates and links.
  • The Roll and color gradingDevelop a whole roll of screenshots at once, flip light and dark mode, and share curves-based looks.
  • Motion Studio and Open CanvasA unified multi-lane timeline, focus pulls, and an infinite pan-and-zoom board of pages.
  • Surface, 3D and printPhysical mockups, WebGL 3D devices, and print-ready PDFs with bleed and crop marks.
  • Chrome extension and Command centerOne-click browser capture into the studio plus a context-aware Cmd-K palette.

In progress

  • Glint Pro rolloutLive Stripe Managed Payments billing and opening Pro beyond the waitlist.
  • Print and board polishTrue PDF TrimBox and BleedBox metadata, CMYK color profiles, and tour links in board exports.
  • Canvas and Roll refinementsOn-canvas handles for tilt-shift bands and per-shot focal points on the Roll.
  • Accessibility passesBetter keyboard paths, focus states, and contrast across new panels.

Exploring

  • Async review on linksOptional pinned comments and sign-off right inside shared pages.
  • Extension element pickerPick any DOM card cleanly and extend capture to Firefox and Edge.
  • Multi-device hero scenesPaired device compositions and more 3D and apparel mockup bases.
  • Public API and CLIHeadless template rendering and multi-language App Store set generation.
