Every release

  • v35.0October 2026

    Contrast

    The third photography codename. This one is about showing the difference: what changed, side by side.

    • Before / After. The new Before / After section in Import puts an old screenshot and the new one in the same frame, split by a divider you drag right on the canvas. Choose left/right or top/bottom, rename the Before and After labels, and change the divider's color and width. Press "Use current as Before" first, then load the new screenshot, and it is set up for you.
    • Works everywhere, exports everywhere. The comparison sits inside your screenshot, so frames, device and 3D mockups, shadows, and filters all apply to it, and it bakes into exported images and videos. It saves with your projects and pages.
    • Animated wipes. Turn on "Animate a wipe" and a Before / After lane appears in Motion Studio. The divider sweeps from the old screenshot to the new one, and you can move or stretch it like any other clip and export it as a video or GIF.
    • Interactive slider pages. "Export interactive slider" downloads one small HTML file with a divider people can drag (or move with the arrow keys). It needs nothing else, so you can put it in a changelog, docs page, or pitch.
    • Simple and Pro modes. A new Simple / Pro switch in the header. Simple shows only the tools most people need, and lists the rest at the bottom of each panel. New visitors start in Simple; if you've used SnapShotPro before, you stay in Pro.
    • A toolbar that follows your selection. Select an arrow, shape, text, or redaction and a small toolbar appears right above it, with color, size, bold and italic, duplicate, bring to front, send to back, and delete. The "⋯" button opens the full right-click menu. Turn it off from Cmd-K if you prefer.
  • v34.0October 2026

    Bokeh

    The second photography codename. This one is about focus: showing people exactly where to look.

    • Spotlight, rebuilt. Add as many focus areas as you need with the Spotlight tool, as boxes, circles, or rounded shapes (hold Shift for a circle). Select one to move it, drag its corner to resize it, or duplicate and delete it like any other object.
    • Blur, soft edges, and color. Everything outside your focus areas can now be blurred as well as dimmed, with a soft-edge slider and any dim color. Spotlights in older projects open exactly as before.
    • Real background blur. The new Bokeh section in Adjust finds the subject of a photo and blurs everything behind it like a wide-open camera lens. Bright spots bloom into round or hexagonal highlights.
    • Private, and in your exports. Subject detection runs in your browser with the same model as Remove Background, so your photo never leaves your computer. Bokeh works inside every mockup, and both effects bake into your exported images.
  • v33.2October 2026

  • v33.0October 2026

    Aperture

    Starting with v33, each release gets a photography codename, in A to Z order. This one is about the small things you do a hundred times a day.

    • Pick colors from anywhere. Every color picker now has an eyedropper that samples any pixel on your screen, including your own canvas (Chrome and Edge). Under each picker, your brand colors and the last 8 colors you used are one click away.
    • Space things evenly. Select three or more objects and Distribute spreads them with equal gaps. Match size makes a selection as wide or as tall as its largest item. Both live in the right-click menu and Cmd-K.
    • Lock and hide that actually hold. Locked or hidden layers can no longer be clicked, dragged, or caught by a marquee or Select all. Hide or lock straight from the right-click menu, and bring everything back with Unlock & show all.

    • Quick export and a tidier stage. Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+S re-exports with the last preset you used, and the preset menu remembers it. Page thumbnails now sit under the canvas instead of squeezing it from the side, and the zoom controls no longer cover the board's Export button.
