Guide

App Store screenshots that convert.

Your screenshots are the biggest lever on your app's conversion rate. Here's how to turn a raw capture into a polished, on-brand, localized set, free in your browser.

1

Get your screenshot in

Open the studio and drag in a screenshot, paste it (Ctrl/Cmd+V), or use Load from URL to pull one straight off the web. Recording a flow instead? Hit Record screen and Glint turns the capture into an editable clip.

Tip: capture at your device's native resolution so text stays crisp when it scales up to store sizes.

2

Frame it in a device

Pick a device mockup (iPhone, iPad, Pixel, MacBook) and your screenshot snaps into a clean, realistic frame. Add a background gradient or mesh, a soft shadow, and a touch of 3D tilt to give the panel depth.

Tip: in a hurry? The AI Art Director takes one prompt ("calm fintech look, navy gradient, iPhone") and composes the whole thing for you.

3

Write captions that sell the value

Switch to App Store Set mode to build a multi-panel story. Each panel gets a short headline that names a benefit, not a feature. "Find your calm in 5 minutes" beats "Meditation timer." Keep them to a few words so they read at a glance on a tiny store thumbnail.

Tip: lead with your strongest panel. Most people never scroll past the first two.

4

Make it on-brand with a Brand Kit

Save your colors, fonts, gradient, device colorway, and logo watermark once as a Brand Kit, then apply it to the whole set in a click so every panel matches. Browse the Community Gallery for a head start, or publish your own.

5

Export a localized set

List the languages you ship in and Glint exports a ZIP with one subfolder per locale, captions AI-translated for each. Drop the folders straight into App Store Connect or Google Play.

Tip: localized screenshots lift conversion in non-English markets more than almost any other single change.

A few principles that matter more than tools

Show the product, not chrome. Crop out status bars and dead space so the actual experience fills the frame. One idea per panel. A screenshot trying to say three things says nothing. Readable at thumbnail size. Preview your set shrunk down; if a headline is unreadable small, it's too long. Consistency reads as quality. Same background, type, and device across every panel signals a polished app before anyone reads a word.

Everything here is free and runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded unless you reach for a cloud feature. See the privacy page for exactly what that means, and the changelog for what's new.
